What "Light Retouching" Includes
Natural skin cleanup, temporary blemish removal, under-eye softening, minor flyaway hair cleanup, color correction, exposure adjustments, and light background cleanup. Advanced retouching (body reshaping, major hair fixes, clothing reconstruction, object removal, or background replacement) is quoted separately.
