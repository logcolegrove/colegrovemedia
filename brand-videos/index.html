04 - How It Works

From First Call to Final Cut

01
Strategy
A working call to map your goals, audience, and what success looks like. Outputs: scope, deliverables, and a shoot date.
02
Pre-Production
Script, shot list, locations, and logistics locked in writing. No surprises on shoot day — you sign off before cameras roll.
03
Production
On-location shoot with cinema camera, lighting, and audio. Calm, efficient set. Interviews handled with care.
04
Post + Delivery
Edit, color, sound, motion graphics. Review cut delivered via Frame.io. Final master + crops via shared link.
