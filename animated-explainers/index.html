03 - How It Works

From First Call to Final Delivery

01
Discovery
A working call to define the message, audience, and goal. We agree on the one thing the viewer must understand and walk away believing.
02
Script + Storyboard
I write the script and storyboard the visual treatment. You review and approve before any animation is built.
03
Animation
2D motion design built shot-by-shot. Voiceover layered in. Iterations happen in review cuts, not in your inbox.
04
Sound + Deliver
Final mix, music, and color polish. Delivered as a master video plus the deliverable crops you need (16:9, 1:1, 9:16).
