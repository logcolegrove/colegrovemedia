03 - How It Works

From First Call to Final Delivery

01
Discovery
A working call to define the story, the audience, and the goal. Together we identify who to interview and what questions actually matter.
02
Prep
Interview questions sent in advance so subjects feel ready. Logistics, location, and schedule locked before anything is filmed.
03
Interview
A calm, conversational setup. Pro audio + 2 cameras. Most people forget the camera is there inside ten minutes.
04
Edit + Deliver
Story-first edit with B-roll, music, and titles. Review cut delivered for feedback, then final master ready to publish.
